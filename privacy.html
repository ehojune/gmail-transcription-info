GMAIL TRANSCRIPTION LOCAL

Privacy policy

Access and purpose

The desktop app requests Gmail read-only permission. That permission covers the mailbox, while the application limits processing to the configured sender, subject prefix, and start time. It reads message headers, message content, attachment metadata, and audio attachments to fulfill transcription requests. Message identifiers track progress and prevent duplicate replies.

Processing and sharing

Audio is downloaded to the owner's computer and transcribed with local speech-recognition software. Transcripts and task information are processed in the owner's Claude session for correction and Korean translation. The connected OpenAI/Codex Gmail assistant coordinates the task and sends the resulting TXT files to the configured original sender. These providers process the information under the owner's account settings and their applicable policies. The desktop OAuth client itself has no Gmail send permission.

The application does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising or training general-purpose models. Data is used only for this user-directed transcription workflow. Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, governs access to Google user data.

Storage and deletion

Audio, transcripts, results, and progress records remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. No automatic deletion period is configured. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Provider-side conversations and sent emails follow the owner's provider retention settings and deletion controls. This public website stores no mail, audio, transcript, or OAuth credential.

Control and contact

The owner may stop the automation, revoke this app in Google Account connections, and delete local working files. Revoking access stops future API access but does not remove existing files or emails. For questions or deletion requests, contact the app owner through the support address displayed on the Google consent screen.
