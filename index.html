Gmail Transcription Local

Turn requested audio attachments into timestamped transcripts, corrected text, and Korean translations.

How it works

  1. The owner authorizes read-only access to their own Gmail account.
  2. The desktop tool selects messages from an owner-configured sender with a specific subject prefix and downloads audio attachments.
  3. Local speech recognition creates a transcript. The owner's existing Claude session corrects it and translates non-Korean audio into Korean.
  4. The connected Gmail assistant sends an acknowledgement, then replies with all completed TXT files together.

This is a private, single-owner workflow. It is not a public upload service. This website provides information only and has no sign-in or file-upload form.
